# Yiiva App Overview

## 📱 What is Yiiva?

Yiiva is a social commerce platform specifically designed for the South African creative community. It bridges the gap between talented local artists and art enthusiasts, providing a beautiful, intuitive space for discovering, sharing, and purchasing authentic creative works.

## 🎯 Mission

To democratize art commerce and give every creator a platform to showcase their work, tell their story, and build a sustainable creative business.

## 🌍 Market Focus

### **Primary Market**: South Africa
- Local currency display (South African Rand)
- Major city locations (Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban)
- Cultural emphasis on South African heritage and contemporary art

### **Target Audience**
- **Creators**: Artists, designers, craftspeople, makers
- **Collectors**: Art enthusiasts, interior designers, gift buyers
- **Supporters**: People who value unique, locally-made products

## ✨ Core Features

### 🏠 **Home Feed**
Instagram-style product feed where users can discover new artists and their creations. Features include:
- Product cards with artist profiles, pricing, and locations
- Like, bookmark, and share functionality
- Category filtering options
- "For You" and "Following" personalized tabs

### 🔍 **Smart Search**
Advanced search functionality that helps users find exactly what they're looking for:
- Search across artists, products, and locations
- Recent searches and trending hashtags
- Real-time filtered results
- Visual masonry grid layout

### 🌟 **Explore Discovery**
Curated discovery experience highlighting the best of South African creativity:
- Featured collections (South African Heritage, Urban Street Style)
- Trending artists with social follow functionality
- Video content showcasing creative processes
- Category-based browsing

### 👤 **Artist Profiles**
Immersive profile pages that tell each artist's story:
- Full-screen hero images
- Comprehensive stats (posts, followers, following)
- Bio and location information
- Categorized product portfolios
- Direct contact and follow options

### 🎬 **Video Experience**
TikTok/Instagram Reels-style video player for creative content:
- Full-screen vertical videos
- Infinite scroll through artist showcases
- Social interactions (like, comment, share)
- Behind-the-scenes creative processes

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### **Visual Identity**
- Clean, minimalist interface that puts artwork first
- Consistent shadows and rounded corners for depth
- Monochromatic color scheme with artwork providing color
- Clear typography hierarchy

### **User Experience Principles**
1. **Artist-Centric**: Every interaction leads back to the creator
2. **Discovery-First**: Multiple pathways to find new content
3. **Mobile-Optimized**: Touch-friendly with haptic feedback
4. **Social**: Familiar patterns from popular social platforms

## 🔮 Vision & Future

Yiiva aims to become the premier destination for authentic South African creativity, eventually expanding to:
- Full e-commerce capabilities with secure payments
- Artist analytics and business tools
- Community features and messaging
- Global marketplace expansion

## 📊 Success Metrics

- **Artist Engagement**: Profile views, follows, contact requests
- **Content Discovery**: Search usage, category filtering, video views
- **Social Interaction**: Likes, bookmarks, shares across content
- **Market Growth**: New artist registrations and geographic spread
