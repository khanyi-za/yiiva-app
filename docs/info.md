## For Visual Artists

### Current Pain Points

- People discover your work through content... but don't know where or how to buy.
- You're stuck juggling **commissions, prints, originals, and orders** — manually.
- Updating a website or managing an online store is **not your focus**.
- You're losing revenue from missed DMs and poor visibility into what fans want.

### Solutions

**1. Turn Your Feed into a Gallery — That Sells**
Each artwork you post becomes part of your **live portfolio**. Viewers can buy prints, originals, or commission work — directly from the post.

**2. Simple Commission Booking**
Our tool lets fans **request commissions via content** — with built-in forms, upfront payment options, timelines, and milestones. No more back-and-forth DMs.

**3. Organize Art by Collection**
Group works by series (e.g. "Urban Portraits," "2024 Oils") and tag your content accordingly — creating a **narrative-driven catalogue** that reflects your style and growth.

**4. Print Fulfillment Integration**
Offer print-on-demand — you upload once, and we handle the rest. Focus on creation, not logistics.

**5. Boost Discovery via Content Search**
Fans can search your feed for "portraits," "charcoal," or "framed prints" — just like they would on an online store — except it's **your content they're exploring**.

**6. Highlight Timelapse or Process Videos**
When fans fall in love with your process, they're more likely to buy. With our tools, your **process content becomes a sales driver** — and you can upsell originals or prints from that very video.

**7. Insightful Audience**
Which content styles drive sales? What formats get commissions? You'll know what works, and what to create more of.
